How to Play

The idea

Drive as far and as fast as you can through highway traffic. One crash ends the run, so weave carefully. Your score grows with distance, and grows faster the quicker you drive.

Controls

Steer
Phone: hold ◀ or ▶. PC: A D or the arrow keys.
Gas
Phone: hold GAS. PC: W or ↑. Turn on Auto accelerate in Settings to drive without holding gas.
Brake
Hold BRAKE, or S / ↓.
Nitro
Hold NOS, or Shift / Space. The blue bar under the speedometer shows what is left. It refills slowly, and blue canisters on the road refill it fast.
Pause
Tap ☰ or press Esc for Resume, Restart and Main menu.

Scoring

Speed bonus
Above 100 km/h every metre counts for more: double at 200 km/h, triple at 300.
Near miss
Pass close beside a car at over 100 km/h. Squeeze past with less than a metre to spare for a Close call, worth double. Near misses in a row build a combo, up to x10. The combo resets if you go 3 seconds without one.
Wrong way
In Two Way mode, drive in the oncoming lanes for double score. Near misses with oncoming cars are worth even more.
Coins
Gold coins on the road are worth 5 each. You also earn 1 coin for every 250 points at the end of a run.

Modes

One Way
All traffic drives your way. Endless.
Two Way
The left two lanes come at you. Endless, high risk, high reward.
Time Attack
60 seconds on the clock. Each green checkpoint gantry adds 30 seconds.

Garage and goals

Spend coins in the Garage on faster cars, engine, nitro and handling upgrades, and new paint. Check Daily Goals for three bonus challenges that change every day.

Tips

Brushing the guard rail slows you down but will not wreck you. Watch for flashing orange indicators: that car is about to change lanes. Trucks and buses are long, so give them room.
